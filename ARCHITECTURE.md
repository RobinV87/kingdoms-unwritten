# Architecture

A world is a serializable value containing its seed, RNG state, tile grid, settlements, clock and structured events. `engine/world.ts` owns world generation and fixed daily simulation and has no browser dependencies. Geography determines production; stocks buffer seasonal food deficits; hunger changes stability and monthly demographics. React schedules whole logical days and displays state. Pixi renders the map independently. Dexie stores complete world snapshots in versioned IndexedDB records. The minimal loop is: generate a seed, inspect settlements, advance time, observe production and consequences, then save or export and resume.

Daily updates must be deterministic and independent of frame rate. Simulation code never uses `Math.random`. Stocks never go below zero, and resource storage and population are capped. Saves must be validated before use. Later systems should be added to the engine as explicit state transitions, not UI effects.

Trade runs as a deterministic daily engine step. Every ten days towns compare stocks with reserve targets and seek reachable surplus offers. A deal reserves the seller's goods and the buyer's gold or barter cargo immediately; the serialized caravan owns those assets until arrival, when both sides receive payment. Routes avoid water and use terrain-weighted travel times. Prices are bounded functions of stock and reserve targets. The map reads caravan progress without changing simulation state. Old saves lazily receive treasuries and an empty caravan list.

Town governance uses aggregate houses (four residents each), household savings, a treasury, and per-capita tax and wage transfers every 30 days. Housing construction consumes timber and stone and transfers treasury wages to households. Housing shortages and high taxes reduce stability. Asking-price policies multiply the bounded supply/demand price for new trades. Optional fields preserve older saves, and imported governance fields are validated.

Grain petitions are assessed after all towns complete the daily food update. A town can have one active case. Each case counts days with an unmet ration and records its decision in the Chronicle. Relief uses the trade engine’s path, duration, payment escrow and delivery step. The council reads closed cases from dated outcome events. Granary construction consumes town resources on commissioning and changes the food storage ceiling only on completion.
